# Ingolstadt 1792

![Ingolstadt 1792](preview.png)

> Ingolstadt, November 1792. One in the morning. Rain on the attic panes, the candle nearly burnt out. Victor Frankenstein has built everything he can. The rest has to come from the storm. AI and Omarchy put the laboratory in our hands. So build. Experiment.

An Omarchy theme in plum night, moonlit lavender, candle amber and lightning cyan, sampled from the painting.

## Backgrounds

**1 · Ingolstadt, 1792.** November, one in the morning. The attic laboratory on the night of the spark.

**2 · Orkney, 1795.** Three years later, alone on a rock in the North Sea, Victor sinks his second creature at three in the morning, still telling no one. We build alone too now, one person and AI. We just don't keep it secret.

**Out of all of us.** Mary Shelley wrote in 1831 that invention "does not consist in creating out of void, but out of chaos." AI is built the same way, from everything people have written and made. Not out of void, but out of all of us.

## Install

Omarchy menu → Install → Style → Theme, then paste:

```
https://github.com/nathankramm/omarchy-ingolstadt-1792-theme
```
